## Summary

Bastion Brief is designed to collect as little personal information as possible.

- We do not use advertising trackers or third-party analytics.
- This website does not set cookies of its own.
- We only receive personal information you choose to send us, such as through the contact form or newsletter sign-up.
- Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers.

## Who we are

This website is operated by the owner of Bastion Brief ("we", "us", "our"). You can contact us about privacy matters through the [contact page](/contact/).

## Information we collect

### Information you send us

**Contact form.** If you use the contact form, we collect your name, email address, chosen subject and message, so that we can respond to your enquiry. The form also records the page it was sent from and how long the page was open before submission, which helps us filter automated spam.

**Newsletter.** If you subscribe to the newsletter, we collect your email address and the page you subscribed from, so that we can send you the newsletter.

These forms only transmit information when they are connected to a delivery service. While a form is not connected, it tells you so, and nothing you type is sent or stored.

### Information collected automatically

**Server logs.** Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically record technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, the pages you request (including any search terms in the address of the search page), the referring page and the date and time of your visit. These logs are used to operate and secure the website.

**Site search.** Searches on this website are processed in your own browser using a search index downloaded from our server. Your search terms are not sent to a third-party search provider. They do appear in the address of the search results page, which may be recorded in server logs as described above.

**Local browser features.** Interactive features such as the checklists in our articles run entirely in your browser and do not save or transmit your selections.

## How we use information

We use personal information only to:

- respond to messages you send us;
- send the newsletter you subscribed to;
- operate, maintain and secure the website;
- comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for advertising profiling.

## Legal bases

Where data protection laws such as the GDPR or UK GDPR apply, we rely on your **consent** for the newsletter, our **legitimate interests** in responding to enquiries and operating a secure website, and **legal obligation** where applicable.

## Third-party services

- **Hosting provider:** stores and serves the website and may process server logs.
- **Google Fonts:** when you load a page, your browser requests font files from Google. Google receives your IP address and browser information as part of this request. See Google's privacy policy for details. The site owner may choose to self-host fonts in future to remove this connection.
- **Form and email providers:** if the contact form or newsletter is connected to a service provider, that provider processes the information you submit on our behalf.
- **Social sharing links:** share buttons are simple links. No data is sent to social networks unless you click one, at which point you are taken to that network and its own privacy policy applies.

## Retention

We keep contact form messages for as long as needed to deal with your enquiry and any follow-up. We keep newsletter subscriber information until you unsubscribe. Server logs are retained according to our hosting provider's standard practices.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal information, to withdraw consent, and to data portability. To exercise these rights, contact us through the [contact page](/contact/). You may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

## Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information we hold, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), strict browser security headers, and minimising what we collect. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

## Children

This website is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the website changes. The date at the top of the page shows when it was last updated.
