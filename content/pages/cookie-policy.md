## What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that websites store in your browser. They can be used to remember preferences, keep you logged in, or track your activity across websites. Similar technologies include local storage, pixels and device fingerprinting.

## Cookies on Bastion Brief

**This website does not set any cookies of its own**, and it does not use analytics, advertising or social media tracking technologies. Because we do not use non-essential cookies, we do not display a cookie consent banner.

Interactive features — such as the search, the article checklists and the forms — run in your browser without storing cookies or tracking identifiers.

## Third-party connections

### Google Fonts

To display our typefaces, your browser downloads font files from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com). Google states that its Fonts service does not set cookies, but your browser does share your IP address and standard request information with Google when fetching the files. See Google's own policies for how it handles this data.

### Form and newsletter providers

If the contact form or newsletter is connected to a third-party service, submitting a form sends the information you entered to that service. Submitting a form does not, by itself, cause our website to set cookies.

### External links and share buttons

Our share buttons are plain links. No connection is made to social networks unless you click one. Once you visit another website, that website's own cookie policy applies.

## Your hosting provider

Our hosting provider may use strictly necessary technologies to deliver and protect the website, such as protecting against abuse or denial-of-service attacks. These do not track you across other websites.

## Managing cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Because this website does not rely on cookies, blocking them will not affect how it works.

## Changes to this policy

If we ever introduce cookies or similar technologies — for example, privacy-respecting analytics — we will update this policy and, where required by law, ask for your consent first.
