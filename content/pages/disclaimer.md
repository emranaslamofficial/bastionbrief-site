## Educational and defensive purpose

Bastion Brief publishes cybersecurity content for **educational and defensive purposes only**. Our articles describe how threats such as phishing, social engineering, malware and account takeover work so that readers can recognise, prevent and respond to them.

Nothing on this website is intended to help anyone gain unauthorised access to accounts, devices, networks or systems, and we deliberately do not publish operational instructions for doing so.

## Authorisation is required

Testing the security of any system, account or network that you do not own, or do not have explicit written permission to test, may be illegal in your jurisdiction — even if your intentions are good. Content about ethical hacking and penetration testing on this website describes professional, authorised practice. You are solely responsible for ensuring that any activity you undertake is lawful and authorised.

## Not professional advice

Our content is general information. It is not legal, financial, technical or professional security advice tailored to your circumstances. If you are dealing with a serious security incident, fraud, identity theft, a business system or a legal matter, seek help from a qualified professional or the relevant authorities.

## Accuracy

We research our articles carefully and update them when platforms or guidance change. However, technology, platform settings, laws and threats change frequently, and we cannot guarantee that every article is complete, accurate or current at the time you read it. Always check official sources — such as a platform's own help centre — before acting on instructions, especially for account recovery.

## No guarantee of security

Following the advice on this website can significantly reduce your risk, but no set of measures can guarantee that an account, device or system will never be compromised.

## External links

We link to external websites, including official help centres, government agencies and standards bodies, for your convenience and further reading. We are not responsible for the content, accuracy or practices of external websites.

## Trademarks

## Account recovery

Bastion Brief cannot access, recover or restore any account on your behalf. Only the platform concerned can do that through its official process. Anyone claiming to recover accounts for a fee is very likely a scammer.
